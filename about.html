ABOUT SMARTEMS

Technology designed
for critical moments.

SmartEMS is a smart emergency medical response platform designed to connect emergency requests, intelligent triage, ambulance dispatch and hospital coordination through one connected system.

OUR PURPOSE

Making emergency response more connected.

During an emergency, unnecessary steps can create delays. SmartEMS focuses on simplifying the emergency activation process and connecting important response stages through a digital platform.

The system is designed to support emergency severity assessment, ambulance allocation, hospital selection and real-time tracking.

🚑

One connected emergency response system.

From emergency activation to ambulance tracking, SmartEMS brings the major response stages together.

OUR APPROACH

Combining software, data and intelligent systems.

SmartEMS combines a web-based interface, backend services, location information and machine learning to create a connected emergency response workflow. The goal is to reduce unnecessary manual steps and provide useful information to users and administrators.
